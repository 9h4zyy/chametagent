General Questions
1. What is Chamet?
Answer: Chamet is a social video-chat platform built around livestreaming and video chat. Users can join 1v1 video chats, use random matching, watch live streams, and join public Party Rooms made by the host. Chamet also offers features such as real-person verification, regional and language matching, virtual gifts, and moderation tools.
2. Is Chamet trustworthy?
Answer: Chamet uses real-person verification, encrypted video calls, AI moderation, and in-app reporting tools to help keep interactions safer. For extra protection, never share passwords, OTP codes, bank-card details, or other sensitive information with people you meet online.
3. Where is Chamet available?
Answer: Chamet connects users from different countries and regions, but especially popular in Asia, Africa, and America. Your country or region is determined by your mobile IP location and the country linked to your phone number.
4. Can I use Chamet on my computer or desktop?
Answer: Yes. You can use Chamet on a desktop browser through the official Chamet platform. Random video chat is also optimized for desktop browsers, so you can use it on a PC without installing a separate desktop program.
5. Is my personal information safe?
Answer: Chamet uses multi-layer encryption, and video calls are protected by end-to-end encryption and real-time safety monitoring. For your own security, never share passwords, OTPs, bank-card details, or other sensitive information in chats. If you notice suspicious activity, change your password and contact support.
6. Can I use the same Chamet account on more than one device?
Answer: Yes. Your chat history and Diamond balance are synchronized across supported platforms. You can switch between a mobile device and a computer by logging in to the same Chamet account.
7. How does Chamet match me with other users?
Answer: For private 1v1 matching, Chamet uses preferences such as language and region. For random video chat, you can use the Match feature to connect with available hosts.
8. Is it safe to talk to strangers on Chamet?
Answer: Chamet uses encryption, moderation, verification, blocking, and reporting tools to support safer conversations. If someone makes you uncomfortable, block or report that person and avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

4. How do I register on Chamet?
Answer: You can create a Chamet account using Google, Facebook, or your phone number. After registration and verification, you can start using the platform’s video-chat features.
5. What information do I need to register on Chamet?
Answer: You can register using Google, Facebook, or a phone number. Account verification may also be required, including face verification in certain situations.
6. Can I register multiple Chamet accounts?
Answer: You may create more than one account, but one face can only verify one Chamet account, and some tasks or free-Diamond rewards may appear on only one account. If you delete a verified account, you must wait 90 days before the same facial data can be used for another account.
7. Can I delete my Chamet account?
Answer: Yes. Go to Settings > About Us > Delete Account. After requesting deletion, you have 14 days to log back in and recover the account. After that, the deletion becomes permanent. Facial data cannot be reused for a new account for 90 days.
8. Why is my Chamet account banned?
Answer: Accounts may be penalized or banned for breaking Chamet’s safety rules. Prohibited behavior includes nudity, improper clothing, sexual gestures, certain inappropriate postures, and minors appearing on camera. Repeated or serious violations can lead to stronger penalties. If you believe a punishment was incorrect, use the complaint or appeal function.
9. Can I bind my old phone number to a new Chamet ID?
Answer: No. If a phone number has already been linked to a Chamet account, it cannot be linked to a new account even after the old account is deleted. You will need a different phone number for the new account.
10. Can I change my country or region in Chamet?
Answer: No. Your country or region is determined by your mobile IP location and the country linked to your phone number, so it cannot be changed manually.
11. Why can’t I pass Chamet face verification?
Answer: Common reasons include trying to verify more than one account with the same face, poor lighting, a covered face, edited or fake photos, or age-related restrictions. 
Chamet Live Rules & Content
1. Where can I report my problem?
Answer: Use the in-app reporting options for safety or user-related issues in live rooms and private video chats. For account, OTP, recharge, or other technical problems, contact customer support through Chamet’s official platform.
2. What are the livestreaming rules on Chamet?
Answer: Livestreams must not include nudity, underwear or sheer clothing, toplessness, sexual gestures, inappropriate poses, or minors on camera. Your face should also remain clearly visible during a stream. Violations can cause the session to be ended and may lead to an account ban.
3. What if I see someone violate the rules?
Answer: Report the user through the in-app reporting tools. In some situations, you can tap the person’s avatar and use the Warning or report option. If possible, keep relevant evidence, such as screenshots or chat history, so the case can be reviewed.
4. How do I block a user on Chamet?
Answer: Use Chamet’s Block feature on the user you no longer want to interact with. Blocking prevents that person from messaging you, mutually following you, or entering your live stream. The blocked user may still be able to view your public profile.
5. How do I report a scam on Chamet?
Answer: If the scam happened in a 1v1 chat, long-press the relevant messages and report the chat history so the case can be reviewed. Be careful with transactions made privately outside the app, because they may not be verifiable by Chamet.
6. How does Chamet’s moderation work during live sessions?
Answer: Chamet uses automated moderation to monitor live video content. If inappropriate content is detected, the connection may be ended immediately. Repeated violations can lead to stronger account penalties.
7. What should I do if someone harasses me on Chamet?
Answer: Block the person and use the report tools available in the call or chat interface. Blocking prevents that user from messaging you, mutually following you, or entering your room.
8. What happens if I am penalized during a 1v1 video chat?
Answer: The system may cut a call if it detects prohibited content such as nudity or minors. Repeated violations may result in a permanent ban. If you believe the penalty was a mistake, use the complaint or appeal process.
Chamet Agency Withdrawal Questions & Help
1. How can I withdraw my Chamet agency commission?
Answer: For agency commission, bind a Metwallet account in the Agency dashboard or Chamet app. Once your accumulated commission reaches $10, the system automatically transfers it to the bound Metwallet account. Host earnings are processed on a weekly settlement cycle and can be withdrawn after you’ve also bound a MetWallet account.
2. Is my withdrawal account information safe?
Answer: Chamet uses compliant payment gateways and encrypted transactions for recharge payments. For account safety, use only official Chamet payment and account pages, and never share your password or OTP codes with anyone.
3. What withdrawal methods are available for Chamet agency commission?
Answer: Withdrawal methods vary by country, but in general MetWallet supports bank transfer, digital wallet, and cryptocurrencies. For the complete information, please visit your own MetWallet account.
4. How do I recharge my Diamonds on Chamet?
Answer: Open the Wallet or recharge area and follow the available payment instructions. You can use official Chamet payment options, including the official web portal and Google Play for Android. Available methods may vary by location.
5. Can I change my payment currency?
Answer: For Google Play, change your country settings to match your billing preference. For other payment methods, the currency is determined automatically based on your IP address.
6. What should I do if my agency commission withdrawal fails?
Answer: For agency commission and host earning, first make sure your Metwallet account is correctly bound and your unpaid commission has reached at least $10. If both conditions are met and the transfer still fails, contact Chamet customer support through an official channel.
7. What is the minimum agency commission required before transfer?
Answer: Your accumulated agency commission must reach $10 before it is sent. If the unpaid amount is below $10, it will remain pending until you reach the minimum.
8. When are Chamet host earnings processed?
Answer: Host earnings are processed weekly to the MetWallet account, typically every Thursday.
9. What should I do if my Diamonds do not arrive after a recharge?
Answer: The payment may still be processed through Google or your bank. Wait up to 24 hours. If the Diamonds are still missing after that, contact customer service and provide the GPA number from your payment record.
Chamet Agency
1. How can I earn money as a Chamet agent?
Answer: Agents can earn commission from the activity of hosts and sub-agents in their network. Agency commission includes Host Commission and Sub-agent Commission, and the commission ratio is based on total earnings over the most recent 30 days. Agents can also use the Agency dashboard to invite and manage members.
2. How can my Chamet agency earn commission?
Answer: Hosts can earn through virtual gifts and video-chat requests. A strong pickup rate, good interaction quality, and active participation can help increase earnings.
3. How often is Chamet agency commission paid?
Answer: Host earnings are processed weekly, typically every Thursday. Agency commission is transferred automatically to a bound Metwallet account once the accumulated commission reaches $10.
4. How can I improve my Chamet agency performance?
Answer: Improve how you engage with users, use an attractive and authentic cover photo, stream consistently, use good lighting and a clean background, and showcase talents such as singing or dancing. Host training is also available to help improve engagement and gift-receiving potential.
5. What are the benefits of becoming a Chamet agency?
Answer: Hosting 1v1 live chats can help you build deeper connections with users and receive higher tips. Hosts can also earn through gifts and requests, grow an audience through consistent activity, and use training resources to improve their performance.
6. What are the benefits of being an agent?
Answer: Agents can build a team, invite hosts and sub-agents, track host activity and chat duration, review commission information, and earn commission from their network.
7. Can I become a Chamet agency without previous agency experience?
Answer: Previous hosting experience is not a necessary requirement. Chamet provides tutorials and training for hosts and agents, which can help new users learn how to engage with viewers and improve their performance.
8. How can I increase my Chamet agency commission?
Answer: Hosts can improve earnings by staying active consistently, using a real and attractive cover photo, maintaining good lighting, showcasing useful talents, keeping a high call rate, and following platform rules. Agents can grow and manage their team, track host and sub-agent performance, invite new team members, and use the Agency dashboard to monitor commission sources.
9. How do I use the Chamet Agency Dashboard?
Answer: The Agency dashboard lets you track 1v1 chat duration and host activity, get invitation links, review invited members, and check commission records. To view invited members, go to My Invitation > Invitee List > Hosts List or Agent List. For earnings, use Invitation Rewards > Regular Rewards for host earnings and Extra Rewards for sub-agent earnings.
10. How do I invite new users to my Chamet agency?
Answer: Log in to the Agency dashboard, copy your agency invitation link, and send it to the person you want to invite. The new user should register through that link so they are connected to your agency.
11. How do I invite a sub-agency to join my team?
Answer: Use the agency invitation link found in the Agency dashboard under the option for inviting Chamet agents, then send that link to the person or sub-agency you want to add.
12. How is my Chamet agency commission ratio calculated?
Answer: Your commission ratio is determined by total earnings over the most recent 30 days. That total includes host commission and sub-agent commission.
