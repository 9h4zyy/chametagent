CHAMET APP & ACCOUNT

Can I delete my Chamet account?

DETAILED ANSWER

Answer: Yes. Go to Settings > About Us > Delete Account. After requesting deletion, you have 14 days to log back in and recover the account. After that, the deletion becomes permanent. Facial data cannot be reused for a new account for 90 days.
